# Reading path: discovery and operating a useful AI service

Use this as optional, question-led reading. It fits inside the existing mission or project-practice allocation. Reading is not a prerequisite gate and does not advance an attempt's evidence state. The app should show the source and its question together, then return the learner to a task.

Dan's playbook supplies the organising framework; Jon's selected writing deepens operating practice; Andrew Dunn's course adds client discovery and decision-making examples. These sources support the existing missions rather than creating three separate courses.

## Read to answer a specific remaining question

- **What counts as approval?** [Human involvement and the approval queue](https://jonjones.ai/ai-automation/daily-wednesday-wisdom-human-in-the-loop-ai-agent-2026-10-21/) [JJ6]. Draw the point where a required decision actually stops an action. Keep ordinary notifications separate.
- **What exactly is the client buying?** [Three purchasing arrangements](https://jonjones.ai/business/ai-automation-services/) [JJ8]. Compare who operates the system, pays its ongoing costs, and can take over or exit. Do not adopt the article's prices as a local benchmark.
- **What would make a small paid build convincing?** [Build-versus-hire and a paid trial](https://jonjones.ai/business/hire-ai-developers/) [JJ11]. Describe a finished result that another person can inspect. Identify the missing skill or evidence you need to deliver it.
- **How do I learn the technical vocabulary?** [A producer/worker learning account](https://jonjones.ai/ai/six-months-from-hackathon-to-production/) [JJ12]. Explain the observed problem, the proposed pattern, and the test that would demonstrate improvement.
- **How could I package a repeatable deliverable?** [AI and consulting](https://jonjones.ai/business/ai-and-consulting-solopreneurs-guide-2026/) [JJ9]. Define one outcome, scope, client contribution and turnaround. Price after understanding delivery and customer value.

## Keep as background or later examples

The [autonomous agents guide](https://jonjones.ai/automation/autonomous-ai-agents-guide/) [JJ1], [agency model](https://jonjones.ai/business/ai-automation-agency-autonomous-systems-2026/) [JJ2], and [agentic solopreneur guide](https://jonjones.ai/automation/agentic-ai-solopreneurs-guide/) [JJ3] provide the wider ambition and example architecture. Pair them with the newer operating accounts above.

The [AI Playbook PDF](https://jonjones.ai/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/The-AI-Playbook-JonJones-AI.pdf) [JJ4] is most relevant if the chosen project concerns content, distribution or email marketing. Treat its stack as an example. Buying every listed tool is not an assignment.

The [agency operating article](https://jonjones.ai/business/building-ai-agency/) [JJ13] connects a narrow deliverable to logs and operating responsibilities. Its calendar thresholds are examples, not readiness gates.

## How to use a source well

After reading, save three short notes: the idea worth trying; what it assumes about this business; and the smallest test that could support or challenge it. An implementation example, a first-person performance claim, and a verified result in your own project should have different evidence labels.

Use current official documentation for exact tool behaviour. [Anthropic's architecture explanation](https://www.anthropic.com/research/building-effective-agents) [T2] and [Claude Code memory documentation](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/memory) [T3] support the design lessons; they do not determine which service Martin should sell.

## Andrew Dunn: discovery and the client decision

The [AI consulting course](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5aZZNskTsx0) [AD1] is an optional source for M01, M02-05, M04-04 and M05. Martin supplied the full transcript; our assessment uses that text, not a verified viewing of the on-screen demonstrations.

Focus on the discovery-interview, opportunity-matrix, client-validation and presentation sections. After reviewing a relevant part, answer one question in the current project: what does the owner need; what does the operator actually do; which assumption could change the preferred option; or what decision should the client make next?

The five planned practice briefs are in `consulting-practice-backlog.md`. The detailed assessment and corrections are in `../docs/andrew-dunn-review.md`. Do not assign the whole video as compulsory homework or import its fee minimums, campaign outcomes, model rankings or revenue forecasts into pass criteria.

The technical demonstrations can later support questions about verified booking, current policy and workflow errors. They are illustrations to examine, not evidence that Martin has tested a production integration. The current course already covers those principles in L04–L06.
